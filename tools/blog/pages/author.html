I'm Dhruv Samant, and I build RigSight on my own. It's a free and open source PC monitor for Windows that records your PC's temperatures, frame rates, crashes, fans, drives, memory and internet use all day, and keeps them as history on your PC.

It started for a simple reason. Tools like HWiNFO showed me every sensor in my PC, but only while I was looking. I wanted to know what my PC did while I wasn't, like how hot it got in last night's game or what was running when it crashed. The first post on this blog, day one, tells that story.

How I write here

  • Every number from my PC comes from RigSight's recorded history, and I check it before it goes in. A number that looks wrong stays out until I can explain it. Two that didn't make it are in My PC reported 146 GB of VRAM.
  • Every claim about what RigSight does is checked against the released app, not a version I'm still working on.
  • When something isn't known, like a game's PC requirements before they're announced, I say so instead of guessing.
  • General facts link to their source, like NVIDIA, AMD or Microsoft.

How RigSight measures explains how each kind of number is recorded. RigSight's code is on GitHub, and the app is at rigsight.app.
