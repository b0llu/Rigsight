Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Everything RigSight records stays on your PC. There is no account, no ads and no usage tracking, and none of your readings, apps or history are ever sent anywhere. The only thing the app does online on its own is ask GitHub whether there's a new version, and you can turn that off.

What RigSight records

To show you your PC's history, RigSight records your temperatures and other sensor readings, fan speeds, which apps you used and for how long, how much each app downloaded and uploaded, your drives, crashes and shutdowns from Windows' own records, and changes to your PC such as driver and Windows updates.

It never records what you type, what's on your screen, the websites you visit or the contents of your files.

Where it's kept

All of it is kept in a file in your own Windows user folder, on your PC. Nobody else, including us, can see it. You choose how long history is kept in Settings, and you can clear it all from there at any time.

The one thing it checks online

With Update automatically on (Settings, About), RigSight asks GitHub for the latest version about once a day. Turn it off and, from version 0.27.0, RigSight never asks by itself: only when you press Check for updates. That request carries RigSight's version number and, like any request on the internet, your IP address. Nothing RigSight has recorded goes with it.

When a new version is out, its installer is downloaded from GitHub: by itself if Update automatically is on, or when you press Update if it's off. GitHub's handling of these requests is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Things that only happen when you click

Some buttons open a web page in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

  • Reporting a problem copies a report to your clipboard and opens a Google Form. You see the report and decide what to paste. Your Windows user folder name is taken out of it.
  • Searching for a crash opens a Google search for that crash.
  • Links to download RivaTuner Statistics Server, to PawnIO's site, and to Buy Me a Coffee open those sites.

During setup

If you choose them, setup installs PawnIO (the driver that lets RigSight read the CPU and motherboard sensors) and RivaTuner Statistics Server (for the overlay in fullscreen games). Windows' own package manager, winget, downloads them from their publishers.

Parts made by others

RigSight includes LibreHardwareMonitor, SQLite and Microsoft's .NET libraries. They run on your PC as part of RigSight and send nothing. The full list is in the third-party notices.

This website

rigsight.app counts visits with Umami, which uses no cookies and keeps nothing that says who you are. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions

RigSight is open source, so you can check all of this in its code. For questions, open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
