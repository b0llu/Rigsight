Crashes

Crashes, explained in plain words.

App crashes, freezes, graphics driver resets, blue screens and sudden shut-offs. RigSight reads them from Windows' own records and keeps what Windows wrote apart from what it concludes.

  1. What Windows recorded

    Crimson Desert crashed on Wed 7 Oct at 10:06 PM, inside nvwgf2umx.dll, a file of NVIDIA's graphics driver.

  2. What RigSight concluded

    The NVIDIA graphics driver failed. Confirmed, because Windows' record names the driver's own file. It also checked what it wasn't: no overheating, memory not full, no new driver in the week before.

  3. What to try

    Update the NVIDIA driver; a clean install helps. If the GPU is overclocked, try stock clocks.

How sure it is. Confirmed means Windows' record itself names the cause. Likely and Possibly are RigSight's reading of the signs around it, and it says so. Not enough to say means the records don't show it.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your PC.

No account. No telemetry. The only thing RigSight checks online is whether there's a new version. Everything else lives in one folder on your PC, and you can clear it with one click.

What it records

  • Which app is in front, and for how long
  • Temperatures, load, clocks, power, fans, memory and drives
  • How much each app downloads and uploads
  • Crashes Windows wrote down, and what changed on the PC

  • What you type
  • What's on your screen
  • What's inside your files
  • The websites you visit

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. RigSight is free and open source under the MIT license. No trial, no premium tier, no ads.

How is it different from HWiNFO or MSI Afterburner?

Hardware monitors like HWiNFO, HWMonitor and MSI Afterburner show what your PC is doing right now. RigSight reads the same kind of sensors but records them all day, so you can open any past day and see what happened, minute by minute.

Can it show how long I use each app?

Yes. RigSight is also a screen time and app usage tracker for Windows: it reports how long each app and game was in use, in the background or minimized, for any day, week, month or year.

Will it slow my games down?

No. On our test PC (Ryzen 7 5700X3D) it uses about 0.02% of the CPU in the background, about half a percent of one core, and runs at low priority, so your games come first.

Does it work with AMD and Intel?

Yes: NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics cards, and Intel and AMD processors.

Will the overlay get me banned?

RigSight never hooks into games. Over borderless and windowed games its overlay is an ordinary window on top. For exclusive fullscreen it uses RivaTuner Statistics Server, the same tool MSI Afterburner uses.

Why does it need admin rights?

Windows only lets programs with admin rights read CPU and motherboard sensors and see each app's internet use. Only the small background part runs as admin, and you're asked once. On a standard account it runs without admin and records what Windows allows.

How much history does it keep?

As much as you like, from three months to forever. A year takes about 100 MB on a PC that's on most of the day.
